Cold Fieldcraft: Observation Technique

With the cold city's routes, cover and supplies, you examine a concrete clue before choosing a route or a practical plan; this reveals no information that the evidence cannot support. Improves the next Cold action that uses this practice. It grants no extra action, automatic hit, free ammunition or new supernatural capability.
