Skills

The skills of The Long Thaw: what each covers and how hard things are.

Aim

Covered: meet this one in play first.

Banter

The first language of every room in Meridian: the line that buys a drink, a pass, a second look.

Covers: the line, the bluff, the trade, the room. Not: staying calm while it fails, which is Cold. Easy: get a stall-holder to name a real price. Medium: make a crew laugh at their own lead. Hard: talk your way past Customs Hall with no paper. Very hard: win a house's Voice over at her own table. Extremely hard: make Sera Howl revise her opinion in front of her crew.

Primary Attribute: Presence

Climb

Shafts, ledges, cable runs and the discipline of carried air.

Covers: shafts, ledges, cable runs, ladders and the air you carry on them; running, sneaking past, moving unseen, and the heat, smoke and cold on the way. Not: picking a lock at the top, which is Tinker. Easy: climb the Service Stair without stopping. Medium: cross a cable run above the Freight Yard. Hard: free-climb the shaft wall for twenty metres. Very hard: make the Lift Head on a half can of air. Extremely hard: climb the Tether's outer frame in the wind.

Primary Attribute: Grit

Cold

Composure under stares, bets and bared teeth. The human who does not flinch is the one they remember.

Covers: composure under stares, threats, bets and a bared set of teeth, and steady hands under pressure: patching a wound, keeping a downed friend breathing. Not: winning the argument, which is Banter. Easy: hold a stranger's stare on the Market Floor. Medium: eat while a crowd watches the human eat. Hard: stand still while a ring bets on your blood. Very hard: take a Veil fighter's warning without a step back. Extremely hard: walk into a house seat alone and sit.

Primary Attribute: Nerve

Scrap

Fighting with what is at hand: fists, a pipe, a blade off a scrap row, the wall behind the other one.

Covers: close fighting with hands and improvised weapons. Not: fired or thrown things, which are Aim. Easy: shove a drunk off a bar stool. Medium: win a pit bout against a green prospect. Hard: hold a stairwell against three loaders. Very hard: beat a ranked Veil fighter in the ring. Extremely hard: beat Tacca Veil on her own floor.

Primary Attribute: Might

Tinker

Founder tech and the city's own: dead panels, locks, lifts, a drone's open casing.

Covers: Founder tech, locks, lifts, panels and drones, and the workings of a rig, a ride or a crawler. Not: charming the clerk who owns the panel, which is Banter; steering, which is Aim. Easy: wake a dead pod lamp. Medium: open a service hatch. Hard: reroute a lift past its stop. Very hard: talk a Vault Drone's casing open and its guns off. Extremely hard: key a Charter door nobody has keyed in three hundred years.

Primary Attribute: Wits
