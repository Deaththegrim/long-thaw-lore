What to say

The exact lines that set off The Long Thaw's systems: travel, Houses, your business, your desk, your crawler, rigs, limbs and the law.

  • Ride the lift: At a lift station. Name where you are going; the fare is taken in the scene.
  • Ride the cage between the gallery and the market: The cage runs between the gallery you woke in and Shaftmouth Market.
  • Cross to another city: At the city's gate or yard, once you know the way.
  • Swear to a House: In a scene, naming the House, before someone with the standing to accept it.
  • Ask your House for a job: At the House's seat, sworn to it, asking an officer.
  • Sell one House out to another: Sworn to two at once: betray one to the other.
  • Go to your premises: Once your business is founded.
  • Run your business: At your premises: hire, fire, buy stock, set prices, pay what you owe.
  • Leave your premises: From inside.
  • Use the bank: Once your business has grown: deposit, withdraw, borrow or repay.
  • Pull a job: Rob, raid or break into somewhere worth it. The law hears about it.
  • Go to your desk: Once you hold a desk at the registry.
  • Work your desk: At your desk: take a docket, file a licence, answer a finding, pay what you owe.
  • Leave your desk: From inside.
  • Pick your crawler's refit: With the yard boss in the scene, once a refit is open.
  • Go aboard your crawler: Once you own one.
  • Work your crawler: Aboard: load cargo, take a haul, buy a manifest, pay what you owe.
  • Climb down from your crawler: From aboard.
  • Mount your rig or ride: With it beside you.
  • Climb out of your rig: While mounted.
  • Charge your rig: At a charging bay.
  • Have your rig repaired: At Wallgate, with the yard boss in the scene, once it has taken wear.
  • Buy the Thunder Cycle: At Wallgate, with the yard boss in the scene.
  • Steal the prototype: At Wallgate.
  • Have a limb fitted: At Varga's Bench, with the fitter in the scene.
  • Have a mod taken out: At the Veil Gym, with the doctor in the scene.
  • Use a built weapon: Once you have built one of that kind.
  • Pay your fine: In the Customs Hall, owing one.
  • Turn yourself in: With the law after you.
  • Bribe your way out: With the law after you.
  • Answer for a killing: In the Customs Hall, carrying infamy.
  • Run from a fight: In a fight. The narrator decides whether you got clear.
